## Third party resources

Some applications or libraries use our API. Here is a non-exhaustive
list of them:

-   [Java wrapper for the universalreading
    API](https://github.com/di72nn/universalreading-api-wrapper) by Dmitriy
    Bogdanov.
-   [.NET library for the universalreading v2
    API](https://github.com/jlnostr/universalreading-api) by Julian Oster.
-   [A plugin](https://github.com/joshp23/ttrss-to-universalreading-v2) designed
    for [Tiny Tiny RSS](https://tt-rss.org/)
    that makes use of the universalreading v2 API. By Josh Panter.
-   [A plugin](https://github.com/Joedmin/xExtension-universalreading-button) designed
    for [FreshRSS](https://www.freshrss.org/)
    that makes use of the universalreading v2 API. By Joedmin.
-   [Golang wrapper for the universalreading
    API](https://github.com/Strubbl/universalreadingo) by Strubbl, for his
    projects [universalreading-stats
    graph](https://codeberg.org/strubbl/universalreading-stats) and the command
    line tool
    [universalreading-add-article](https://codeberg.org/strubbl/universalreading-add-article).
-   Tool to automatically download Universal Reading articles into your local
    computer or Kobo ebook reader
    [wallabako](https://gitlab.com/anarcat/wallabako) by anarcat.
